Established Equipment Rental Business

New Orleans Metro  ·  ~$942K Revenue  ·  Real Estate Included

Business description

This is a rare opportunity to acquire a well-established equipment rental business serving the greater New Orleans market. The company has built a strong reputation over time by providing dependable service to both contractors and retail customers, supported by a diverse fleet of equipment and a highly visible location within a major corridor.

The business generates consistent annual revenue and cash flow, with operations centered around daily and weekly rentals across a broad range of equipment categories. The current ownership has developed a loyal customer base and repeat business relationships, creating a solid foundation for continued success.

The asset base includes a well-maintained fleet of rental equipment, along with supporting infrastructure and systems necessary to operate efficiently. The business is positioned as a turnkey opportunity for a new owner, with established processes already in place.

There is meaningful upside for a buyer looking to grow the business. Opportunities include expanding the fleet mix, increasing utilization rates, enhancing marketing efforts, and capturing additional market share within the surrounding metro area. A strategic buyer or operator with industry experience may also identify synergies and efficiencies that can further improve performance.

The current ownership is open to providing a transition period to support a smooth handoff and ensure continuity with customers and operations.

This opportunity is best suited for an individual operator, existing rental company, or strategic buyer seeking to establish or expand a presence in a strong regional market.
